1. נ״ט בר נ״ט: the governing idea Notes pp. 1-17
Central question When parve food receives only second-hand meat or dairy taste, is that taste still strong 
enough to create בחלב בשר when the opposite type is added?
Core rule: נ״ט בר נ״ט = meat/dairy taste weakened through successive transfers. The halacha follows Shmuel that 
this weak taste can be permitted in בחלב בשר. Tosfos: the leniency is specific to בחלב בשר, where two strong tastes 
must fuse; it does not neutralize an inherently forbidden taste.

the prohibition back into the source bottle?
View / condition Result
Rashi / Rav Chisda line Treat the continuous stream as a halachic connection, so the source 
can be affected.
Rabbeinu Tam line Does not treat נצוק as connecting the source for this prohibition.
S”A / Rama in significant loss Allow reliance on the non-connection view in a genuine great-loss 
case.
Mevushal wine poured into a cup containing forbidden non-mevushal 
wine residue The remaining bottle can still face a נצוק problem unless the 
residue is nullified; מבושל status of the source does not erase an 
already-forbidden receiving residue.

14. Wine left with a non-Jew: seals and ונכנס יוצא Notes pp. 160-179
Situation Rule to remember Why
Open wine left alone with an idolater S”A: prohibited immediately when the access
conditions are met.
There is no need to prove actual touching 
once the protective conditions fail.
Open wine left alone with a Muslim Course rule: prohibited after enough time to 
walk a mil; Shach can be stricter where 
drinking itself is the concern.
Lower libation concern changes the 
time/access analysis.
ונכנס יוצא works Non-Jew does not know you will be gone a 
meaningful time; cannot see you 
approaching; house/area is not locked against
your return.
Fear of immediate return replaces a physical 
seal.
Fixed weekly run/known schedule Undermines ונכנס יוצא because the non￾Jew knows the absence window.
Predictability removes the deterrent.
Conditions fail: open vs closed Open wine can be prohibited immediately; 
closed wine requires enough time to open 
and reseal without detection.
Tamper time matters only when a closure 
must be defeated.
Protective options Double seal; combination/locking closure; 
some rely on a single effective modern seal 
under the Rama; employment consequences 
can create fear; cameras help when the 
worker knows they are being monitored; 
hidden wine is protected.
Each device must create either evidence of 
tampering or credible fear of being caught.
Screw-on metal cap Taught as an effective closure in the tested 
unattended-wine context.
Opening/resealing can leave detectable 
evidence depending on the cap.
15. Final synthesis: five distinctions that organize the course
Ask this first What it separates
Is the source merely absorbed meat/dairy taste, or an inherently 
forbidden substance?
נ״ט בר נ״ט can weaken the first; it does not make the second 
disappear.
Is the source still connected? Continuous cooking or a liquid stream can be treated more 
strictly than a solid already removed from its source.
Did the event happen לכתחילה or already בדיעבד ?Many disputes are about deliberately creating a permitted 
outcome, even when the final food would be permitted after the 
fact.
Is the concern ordinary איסור or סכנה?
Fish/meat uses different reasoning about bitul and deliberate 
nullification.
For wine: who owns/touches, what act occurred, and is there a 
continuous stream or unsupervised access?
יינם סתם rulings change by actor, form of contact, 
ownership/status, נצוק, seals, and opportunity - not by one 
blanket rule.
Use the charts above as the operative review. The synthesis is only an index to the distinctions already developed; it adds no new 
rule.
