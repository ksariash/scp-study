How essay practice works

Build the essay one pairing at a time. For each authority or concept, choose the matching position. You get feedback immediately; a correct pairing is added to the essay and the next one appears automatically. Weak facts return more often; mastered facts appear less often.

Multiple choice study

Return to the adaptive question mode for the 58 multiple-choice questions.

How multiple choice mode works

Answer one question at a time and submit for immediate grading and explanation. Questions you miss, take longer on, or have not seen recently are weighted to return more often. Your progress is saved on this device, and you can filter by category or jump directly to any question from the question-number menu.

Practice test

All 58 questions, shuffled, with a 90-minute countdown. Answer timing and results also feed your study-mode scheduling.

What happens during the test

Each submitted answer is locked and immediately graded with the category and explanation. You may move backward and forward. The test finishes automatically when all 58 questions are submitted or the timer reaches zero.
