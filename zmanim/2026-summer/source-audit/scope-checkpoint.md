## Five green essay scope groups

1. **Fish and meat danger / bitul** - concise pages 6-7. Explicitly test the highlighted positions of Magen Avraham, Divrei Malkiel, Issur V'Heter, Maharil, Pischei Teshuva, Rav Wosner, and Chayei Halevi.
2. **Hot-water transfer / direct stream** - concise pages 9-10. Explicitly test Shach's liquid/solid distinction, Shach/Pri Megadim on what is forbidden by the stream, Rama's direct-stream rule, Shach's disagreement, and Chochmas Adam's practical chumra.
3. **Stam yeinam benefit positions** - concise pages 16 and 18. Explicitly test Rashi/Geonim, Rosh, Rambam, S"A, Rama, and the Gaonim relationship.
4. **Sherry-cask whiskey** - concise pages 25-26. Explicitly test Shach, S"A/Taz, Rav Moshe, Mishnah Halachos, Minchas Yitzchak, and the two highlighted responses to ein mevatlin issur lechatchilla.
5. **Four criteria for full wine-touch prohibition** - concise page 33. This is an unordered-list exercise: Intent to touch; knowledge that the liquid is wine; not being distracted/touching for the sake of the wine; shaking the wine. Four interchangeable roots accept the four criteria, each response is single-use, and credible distractors have no valid edge.

## Multiple-choice grouping rule

The 58-question bank is generated only from yellow scope. A single item may cover several tightly related yellow obligations when each is required to answer the item. Blue-highlighted names are covered only when the learner must identify the name-to-rule relationship correctly.

Green-only content is not counted as multiple-choice coverage unless that same material is independently yellow-highlighted elsewhere.
